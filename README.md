# La Vibe Map

> **Discover Benin through culture, places, experiences and trusted local recommendations.**

## Overview

La Vibe Map is a digital discovery platform focused on helping visitors and residents explore Benin through authentic cultural and tourism experiences. The platform is designed to connect users with places, activities, local experiences and reliable information in a simple, contextual way.

## Problem

Discovering destinations and cultural experiences can be difficult when information is fragmented across social networks, blogs and word of mouth. Visitors may struggle to identify trustworthy places, understand local context and find experiences beyond the most visible tourist destinations.

## Solution

La Vibe Map aims to bring relevant cultural and tourism information together in one digital experience, helping users discover where to go, what to do and how to experience Benin more meaningfully.

## Target Users

- International and regional visitors
- Domestic tourists
- Young people exploring Benin
- Cultural and tourism organizations
- Local experience providers and businesses

## Core Features

- **Interactive discovery** — explore places and experiences
- **Cultural guides** — understand the history and context behind destinations
- **Experience discovery** — find activities, events and local experiences
- **Trusted recommendations** — prioritize useful and verified information
- **Local actor profiles** — connect users with relevant service and experience providers
- **Personalized exploration** — eventually recommend experiences based on interests and location

## Current Status

🟡 **Advanced concept / product design**

The product vision, target users and core experience have been defined. The next step is to validate the most valuable discovery use cases with travelers and local tourism actors.

## Roadmap

1. Map the main user journeys
2. Identify priority destinations and categories
3. Validate information and trust requirements
4. Design the discovery MVP
5. Pilot with selected destinations and local actors
6. Expand coverage and personalization

## Vision

La Vibe Map aims to become a trusted digital gateway to discovering Benin — not just by showing places on a map, but by helping people understand and experience the culture behind them.
